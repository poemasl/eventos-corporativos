The shows

Two shows, one emotion

Born Flamenco

Show directed by José Manuel Álvarez. Pure, intimate flamenco. Guitar, piano, dance and singing in a close, direct format that connects with the soul of authentic flamenco.

Duration: 1 hour · Artists: Guitar, piano, dance and singing

Barcelona Guitar Trio & Dance

A tribute to Paco de Lucía. A journey through the master's work with breathtaking technical mastery and stage presence that leaves you speechless.

Duration: 1 hour · Artists: 3 guitars, dance and percussion
Wednesday to Sunday · 6pm and 8pm
